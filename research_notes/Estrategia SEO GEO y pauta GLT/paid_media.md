# Paid digital media (lead gen + brand awareness) for freight/3PL: evidence for GLT Logistics

Research date: 28 Oct 2026. Method: WebSearch only. Direct fetch of wordstream.com and webtonic.io failed: those hosts are outside the session's network allowlist. Semrush MCP returned "no_api_units", so no Semrush ad-intelligence data could be pulled. Most figures below come from search-result summaries of vendor and agency pages, not from full-page reads. ## Q1. Competitors' observable paid activity (Warp, Go Freight AI, ExFreight, uShip, Freightquote/C.H. Robinson, FreightCenter, BringGo, Nuvocargo, Flock Freight, Loadsmart)

### Takeaway
Public evidence of competitors' current paid activity is thin. The best-documented case is Flock Freight's 2022 brand campaign (TV plus creative-agency work, with award-entry lift figures). Most other "visibility" is earned or semi-earned: FreightWaves coverage, podcasts and webinars. Seeing who bids on which Google keywords requires an ad-intelligence tool (Semrush/SpyFu, Google Ads Transparency Center) or GLT's own Auction Insights. I could not get that data in this session.

- **Flock Freight.** It launched the "Define Your Load" brand campaign with Maximum Effort (Ryan Reynolds' agency) in Nov 2022 ([BusinessWire press release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221110005492/en); [Flock Freight blog](https://www.flockfreight.com/blog/maximum-effort-ad-campaign)). ### Inferences
- The venture-funded digital brokers (Flock, Loadsmart, Nuvocargo, Warp) lean on earned media, FreightWaves relationships, podcasts, webinars and occasional big brand creative. Search ads are not their visible lead channel. The quote-intent marketplaces (Freightquote, FreightCenter, uShip) are the likeliest heavy Google Ads bidders on "LTL freight quote"-type terms, so GLT will face them in the auction.
- For a budget-conscious mid-size 3PL, copying Flock's TV and celebrity-agency approach is out of reach. Copying the podcast, webinar and FreightWaves-PR playbook is feasible at low cost.
- GLT should run its own Auction Insights within 2–4 weeks of launching search campaigns. It should also check the Google Ads Transparency Center (adstransparency.google.com) and the LinkedIn Ad Library (linkedin.com/ad-library) manually for each competitor. Those tools could not be queried here.

### Gaps
- No direct read of the Google Ads Transparency Center or the LinkedIn Ad Library for any of the 10 competitors. The session cannot do interactive browsing, and Semrush had no API units.
- No evidence found on ExFreight's or BringGo's paid activity, or on Meta ads by any competitor.
- No confirmed labelled FreightWaves sponsored content, newsletter sponsorships or event sponsorships for these competitors.

## Q2. 2025–2026 benchmarks: Google Ads, LinkedIn, Microsoft Ads, Mexico (Spanish)

### Takeaway
There is no authoritative, freight-specific public benchmark. For planning, use the following:
- **Google Search:** about $5–7 CPC and about $70–135 CPL for freight-related search. These are vendor proxies; B2B-wide CPLs can run much higher.
- **LinkedIn:** about $5–12 CPC (around $2–3 for thought-leader ads), $28–60+ CPM, and $100–200+ CPL.
- **Microsoft Ads:** CPCs roughly 20–40% below Google.
- **Google Ads in Mexico:** the national average CPC is about $0.66 (Dec 2024). Freight terms are likely higher, but no freight-specific Mexico figure was found.

### Cited Findings
**Google Ads (cross-industry)**
- The WordStream/LocaliQ 2026 benchmark draws on 13,474 search campaigns run April 2025–March 2026 on Google and Microsoft Ads. It reports an average CPC of $5.42 and an average/median CPL of $66.69, the first CPL decline in five years [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([WordStream 2026](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2026-google-ads-benchmarks); summarized by [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/google-ads-cpcs-rose-to-5-42-in-2025-with-87-of-industries-seeing-increases-but-conversion-rates-climbed-to-8-18-as-automation-improves/) and [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ads-costs-rise-again-but-conversions-improve-report-455663)).
- CPC rose from $4.66 to $5.42 between the two reports, with increases in 87% of industries; the average conversion rate was 8.18% ([Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/google-ads-cpcs-rose-to-5-42-in-2025-with-87-of-industries-seeing-increases-but-conversion-rates-climbed-to-8-18-as-automation-improves/)).
- Other aggregators give different numbers: CPC $5.26, 7.5% CVR and $70.11 CPL in one; CPC $4.34 and $76.12 cost per conversion in another. Methodologies differ ([ClickMinded](https://www.clickminded.com/google-ads-benchmarks/); [Digital Applied](https://digitalapplied.com/google-ads-benchmarks-2026/)).

**Google Ads (freight/logistics proxies)**
- WordStream's "Industrial & Commercial" category is the closest published proxy for freight keywords: CPC about $5.87, CVR 8.20%, CPL about $75.19 [VENDOR/AGENCY: as cited by Webtonic, a logistics-marketing agency] ([Webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/trucking-and-logistics-google-ads-statistics)).
- Webtonic's logistics-specific claim is an average CPC of $6.45, a 3.10% search conversion rate and $135 per qualified lead [VENDOR/AGENCY, methodology not stated] ([Webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/trucking-and-logistics-google-ads-statistics)).
- Metadata.io's B2B benchmark puts Google Ads CPL at about $524 for general B2B. That is a B2B SaaS-heavy sample, so treat it as a high-end outlier for freight [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([Metadata B2B benchmarks](https://metadata.io/b2b-advertising-benchmarks)).
- A single-account case for US cargo transport cut CPL from 443 to about 226–230 UAH. This is an anecdote [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([Spilno Agency](https://spilnoagency.com.ua/en/cases-us/google-ads-for-a-cargo-and-oversized-transportation); [Freelancehunt case](https://freelancehunt.com/en/showcase/work/case-promoting-cargo-transportation-services/1836500.html)).
- Webtonic also publishes trucking/logistics LinkedIn and Meta ads statistics pages. They could not be fetched (host blocked) ([Webtonic LinkedIn](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/trucking-and-logistics-linkedin-ads-statistics); [Webtonic Meta](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/trucking-and-logistics-meta-ads-statistics)).

**LinkedIn Ads (B2B, 2025–2026)**
- CPC clusters at $5–12 for B2B and $15–25 for C-suite or enterprise targeting. One source gives an average of $5.26–8.50, ranging from $2.29 (thought-leader ads) to $24+ [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/linkedin-ads-b2b-cost-2026); [ClickMinded](https://www.clickminded.com/linkedin-ads-benchmarks/); [ZenABM 211 companies](https://zenabm.com/blog/linkedin-ads-benchmarks-2026.md)).
- CPM is about $28–60, compared with Meta at about $7–15. Tech/IT audiences run CPC $8–22 and CPM $35–75 [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/linkedin-ads-b2b-cost-2026); [Benly](https://benly.ai/learn/linkedin-ads/linkedin-ads-cost)).
- CPL estimates:
  - Benly: $50 / $95 / $200 (low / mid / high).
  - Foundry CRO: about $110 median.
  - Metadata.io: about $202 for 2025, the most data-backed figure (153 advertisers, $57.6M spend) [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([Benly](https://benly.ai/learn/linkedin-ads/linkedin-ads-benchmarks); [Foundry CRO](https://foundrycro.com/blog/linkedin-ads-benchmarks-by-industry-2026/); [Metadata](https://metadata.io/resources/blog/what-linkedin-ads-cost)).
- CPL by format (2025, Metadata.io): Document ads about $142, Image $200, Video $265, Conversation ads $362. Another source puts document ads with Lead Gen Forms at about $45–65 [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([Metadata](https://metadata.io/resources/blog/what-linkedin-ads-cost); [Factors.ai](https://www.factors.ai/blog/linkedin-ads-cost)).
- Lead Gen Form conversion: estimates range from 5–12% to 4–6%, with click-to-lead measured at 5.9% in 2025. LGF CPL runs about 20–30% below landing-page CPL. Sources conflict [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([ClickMinded](https://www.clickminded.com/linkedin-ads-benchmarks/); [Factors.ai](https://www.factors.ai/blog/linkedin-ads-cost); [Metadata](https://metadata.io/b2b-advertising-benchmarks)).
- LinkedIn costs more per click, but pipeline quality often makes it cheapest per opportunity. This is a vendor opinion ([Factors.ai](https://www.factors.ai/blog/linkedin-ads-cost)).

**Microsoft Ads**
- Microsoft Ads CPCs are claimed to be 20–40% below Google's for equivalent keywords; one source claims about 70% lower ($1.54 vs $2.69) [VENDOR/AGENCY, conflicting] ([TripleDart](https://www.tripledart.com/blog/microsoft-ads-vs-google-ads-for-b2b-saas); [Gravitate](https://www.gravitatedesign.com/blog/microsoft-ads-for-b2b/); [Priority Pixels](https://prioritypixels.co.uk/blog/microsoft-ads-vs-google-ads/)).
- Adding LinkedIn profile targeting (company, industry, job function) on Microsoft Ads reportedly lifts CTR about 16% and CVR about 64%, per Microsoft data cited by a vendor. One source says the targeting was extended to Performance Max in 2026 ([Gravitate](https://www.gravitatedesign.com/blog/microsoft-ads-for-b2b/); [Stackmatix](https://stackmatix.com/blog/microsoft-ads-vs-google-ads)).

**Mexico / Spanish-language Google Ads**
- Mexico's average Google Ads CPC was about $0.66 versus about $7.66 in the US (Statista/Semrush, Dec 2024, all industries) ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1114826/mexico-search-advertising-cpc); [Mercado Negro](https://www.mercadonegro.pe/medios/digital/google-ads-cuales-son-los-10-paises-con-el-costo-por-clic-mas-alto/)).

### Inferences
- **US search planning ranges for GLT:** CPC about $4–8 on non-brand freight terms. "LTL freight quote" and broker terms likely sit at the upper end because the marketplaces compete there. Raw form-fill CPL is about $70–150. The share of those leads that become sales-qualified for a B2B shipper will be well below 100%: many form fills on "freight quote" terms are one-off or consumer shippers.
- **Mexico:** Spanish-language Mexico targeting ("transporte de carga a Estados Unidos", "flete México USA", "agente de carga") is likely several times cheaper per click than in the US. That makes it an efficient test cell for cross-border shippers, though the figure is an all-industry national average.
- **Microsoft Ads:** a cheap add-on (typically 5–15% of search budget) that lets GLT layer LinkedIn company and industry targeting onto search.

## Q3. What works in freight B2B paid media (ABM, thought-leader ads, document ads, retargeting, offline conversion import, budgets, sales cycle, cost per closed account)

### Takeaway
The best-supported practices are:
1. **Offline conversion import.** Feed CRM stages (MQL, SQL, Won) back to Google so bidding optimizes for real shippers, not one-off quote seekers.
2. **LinkedIn ABM.** Use matched account lists, thought-leader ads (cheapest CPC) and ungated or lead-gen-form document ads.

Freight-specific measured results are almost nonexistent publicly. Sales cycles are multi-stakeholder, and CAC must be computed internally; one illustrative 3PL example gives about $10k per customer.

### Cited Findings
- **Thought-leader ads.** ZenABM's analysis of 119 TLAs finds them the best B2B ABM format: median CTR 2.68%, median CPC $2.29 [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([ZenABM](https://zenabm.com/blog/linkedin-thought-leader-ads-for-abm); [ZenABM guide](https://zenabm.com/blog/linkedin-thought-leader-ads-ultimate-guide.md)).
- A Chili Piper interview reports TLA engagement rates of 7.9–8.4% and CPMs of $112–279 at two companies, and says no formal benchmarks exist yet ([Chili Piper](https://www.chilipiper.com/post/linkedin-thought-leader-ads)).
- **Freight-specific thought-leadership case.** Able Transport Solutions, a heavy-haul carrier, ran a thought-leadership campaign that "significantly outperformed" its other brand ads and reached 63% more users. No spend or baseline is given [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([Qwilr case study](https://pages.qwilr.com/Case-Study-Able-Transport-company-name-61NNnrV3TY6j)).
- **Document ads.** These are native in-feed PDFs, gated with a Lead Gen Form or ungated, and LinkedIn reports view-depth and download metrics ([LinkedIn Business](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/ads/sponsored-content/document-ads)). ZenABM recommends ungated documents for ABM trust-building [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([ZenABM ABM ads](https://zenabm.com/blog/abm-ads); [best formats](https://zenabm.com/blog/best-linkedin-ad-formats)).
- **Offline conversion import.** Capture the GCLID (auto-tagging on) and upload CRM stages (MQL / SQL / Closed-won) as separate conversion actions. Assign values as average contract value × stage close rate, then move to Maximize Conversion Value / tROAS. Monitor GCLID match rates. Enhanced Conversions for Leads (hashed email) supplements this [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([Prose Media](https://www.prosemedia.com/blog/offline-conversion-imports-b2b-paid-search); [GrowthSpree OCI 2026](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/google-ads-offline-conversion-import-b2b-saas-2026); [Revvim](https://www.revvim.com/2026/04/01/bidding-on-offline-crm-stages-instead-of-unqualified-b2b-leads/)).
- Sources say OCI and ECL uploads moved to Google's Data Manager API in 2026, but give conflicting dates (April vs 15 June 2026). Verify against Google's documentation ([GrowthSpree](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/google-ads-offline-conversion-import-b2b-saas-2026); [Revvim](https://www.revvim.com/2026/04/01/bidding-on-offline-crm-stages-instead-of-unqualified-b2b-leads/)).
- **Sales cycle.** 3PL deals involve multiple stakeholders: operations, procurement, finance and sometimes IT ([Konstruct Digital](https://konstructdigital.com/digital-marketing/logistics-marketing)). For trade shows, up to 90% of badge scans never become a qualified conversation, and the post-show cycle is long [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([Warematch](https://www.warematch.com/blog/hidden-cost-of-3pl-tradeshows)). The only numeric cycle found is for logistics software, not services: a B2B SaaS median of 84 days, roughly 30–90 days ([SaaSHero](https://www.saashero.net/uncategorized/how-to-market-logistics-software/)).
- **CAC.** Define it as total sales plus marketing spend divided by new customers, including ads, agency fees, commissions and acquisition staff. For contract logistics, use payback months instead. In an illustrative regional 3PL, $200k marketing plus $300k sales spend wins 50 customers, a CAC of $10,000. This is an example, not survey data ([Racklify](https://racklify.com/encyclopedia/how-warehouses-3pls-and-logistics-providers-should-calculate-customer-acquisition-cost-cac/); [Racklify CAC benchmarks](https://racklify.com/encyclopedia/customer-acquisition-cost-benchmarks-for-logistics-and-ecommerce/)).
- **Budget rule.** Logistics firms often set marketing budgets as arbitrary percentages of revenue. One consultancy recommends sizing against what competitors spend instead. No benchmark percentage was found ([Emulent](https://emulent.com/?p=20214)).
- **Platform comparison for 3PL lead gen.** A Dutch 3PL marketplace compares Google Ads with LinkedIn for 3PL lead generation [VENDOR, self-interested] ([Zendeq](https://www.zendeq.com/3pl-lead-generation-in-the-netherlands/)).

### Inferences
**Suggested starting design for GLT**, inferred from the benchmarks above. It is not a sourced plan.

1. **Google Search, about 50–60% of paid budget.** Use tight exact/phrase campaigns on lane and service intent: "LTL Miami", "Miami drayage", "cross-border freight Mexico", "freight to Canada", "3PL Miami". Add brand defense. Use negatives for consumer and household moves. Track quote form, call and CRM stages via OCI.
2. **Microsoft Ads clone, about 5–10%.** Add LinkedIn company/industry overlays.
3. **LinkedIn, about 25–35%.** Upload account lists (shippers by NAICS and size in FL/TX/Southeast with Mexico exposure, plus forwarders and brokers for capacity partnerships). Run thought-leader ads from GLT's leadership, lane and market-update document ads (Lead Gen Form), and retargeting of site visitors.
4. **Mexico Spanish-language search test, about 5–10%.** Run it if GLT can serve MX-origin shippers.

**Example monthly sizing at an illustrative $5k/month:**
- About $3k on Google. At a $6 CPC that is about 500 clicks; at a 3–8% CVR, about 15–40 leads.
- About $1.5k on LinkedIn. At $8 per click that is about 190 clicks; at a 5–10% LGF CVR, about 10–20 leads.
- Treat this as arithmetic on the benchmark ranges above, not a forecast.

**Evaluation:** measure cost per SQL and cost per first shipment, not CPL. Allow at least 90 days before judging LinkedIn, given the multi-stakeholder cycle.

### Gaps
- No public, measured freight-broker or 3PL figures for cost per SQL, cost per closed account, or typical paid budgets of mid-size 3PLs.
- No measured freight sales-cycle length; services-specific data was not found.
- No freight-specific ABM, retargeting or document-ad results beyond the single Able Transport case.

## Q4. Brand-awareness options in freight media: newsletters, events, podcasts, approximate costs

### Takeaway
Trade-media rates are almost entirely "contact sales". FreightWaves explicitly treats its options as a "starting point for a conversation", not a price tag. TIA Capital Ideas (Gaylord Palms, Orlando, 5–7 April 2027) restricts sponsorships to TIA members and requires full payment at booth booking. No public dollar figures were found for FreightWaves, DC Velocity, Logistics Management, JOC or TIA. GLT must request media kits.

### Cited Findings
- **FreightWaves.** Its advertising page says all options are "a starting point for a conversation — not a price tag" and that the team scopes a mix to budget; media sponsorships are among the options. The 2022 FreightWaves Ratings media kit priced custom content on CPC/CPL/CPA models and listed newsletter sponsorship as "coming soon". It is outdated ([FW Ratings 2022 media kit](https://ratings.freightwaves.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/FW-Ratings-2022-Media-Kit.pdf)).
- FreightWaves earns from advertising and sponsorship, SONAR subscriptions and F3 events ([Hypepotamus / profiles](https://www.hypepotamus.com/freightwaves/)). It previously partnered with the WSJ on the WSJ Logistics Report ([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/?p=242129)).
- **DC Velocity.** Published by AGiLE Business Media; runs weekly e-newsletters. No 2026 rate card was found ([DC Velocity about](https://www.dcvelocity.com/about)). **Logistics Management** is published by Peerless Media; no 2026 rates were found ([Peerless LM report](https://www.peerlessmedia.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/LM_tap_report.pdf)).
- **TIA Capital Ideas 2027.** Gaylord Palms, Orlando, 5–7 April 2027. Sponsorships are limited to TIA members. Booth allocation follows TIA Priority Points, and full payment is due at reservation. The 2025 sponsorship contact was Valerie Sumner. Prices were not visible in retrieved text ([TIA 2027 exhibitor guidelines](https://tianet.org/common/Uploaded%20files/2027%20CIC%20Exhibitor%20Guidlines.pdf); [TIA 2025 exhibit page](https://tianet.org/meetings/2025exhibit)).
- **Trade-show ROI caution.** Up to 90% of scanned contacts never become a qualified sales conversation [VENDOR/AGENCY] ([Warematch](https://www.warematch.com/blog/hidden-cost-of-3pl-tradeshows)).
- **Podcasts.** Freight execs commonly appear as guests on The Logistics of Logistics, Inbound Logistics and Everything is Logistics, which is earned rather than paid exposure ([Metacast](https://metacast.app/podcast/the-logistics-of-logistics/YWsBAvqW/a-decade-of-loadsmart-a-look-back-to-move-forward-with-felipe-capella/S4eQqPfR); [Inbound Logistics podcast](https://inboundlogisticspodcast.libsyn.com/navigating-the-changing-landscape-of-transportation-insights-from-loadsmart-guest-george-swartz); [Everything is Logistics](https://digitaldispatch.buzzsprout.com/1421218/10898280-where-is-the-logistics-industry-headed?t=0)).
- **FreightWeek** (freightweek.org) is a separate, non-US publication. It publishes a 2026 media pack with e-newsletter rates, but it is not FreightWaves and is likely irrelevant to GLT's US-MX-CA audience ([FreightWeek media pack 2026](https://www.freightweek.org/images/PDF/media_pack/mediapack2026.pdf)).

### Inferences
