## US-Mexico cross-border trade and trucking in 2026 (volumes, nearshoring, Laredo, tariffs/USMCA, MVE, Carta Porte, cross-border LTL, search demand)

### Takeaway
US-Mexico freight is growing at double digits year over year in 2026, and trucks and Laredo carry most of it. Mexico was the top US goods partner in 2025 at about $872.8B. The USMCA was not renewed at the 1 July 2026 joint review, so it now goes through annual reviews, but USMCA-qualified goods keep preferential treatment. Mexico's electronic customs value declaration (MVE, E2 format) became mandatory on 1 Oct 2026 after several postponements. Together these create urgent, compliance-driven search demand in Spanish and English.

### Cited Findings
- **2025 baseline:** US-Mexico goods trade reached a record $872.8B in 2025, which made Mexico the top US goods trading partner, ahead of Canada ($712.8B) and China — [CRS IF11175 (Aug 2026)](https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2026-08-13_IF11175_366b72c3fd2f3ebe10b28bf3feddca0088251bbb.html); [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/us-mexico-trade-hits-new-high-of-872b-in-2025). USTR puts the figure at $871.6B and the goods deficit at $197B (+16.9%) — [USTR](https://ustr.gov/countries-regions/americas/Mexico). Goods plus services came to $976.1B — [CRS](https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2026-08-13_IF11175_366b72c3fd2f3ebe10b28bf3feddca0088251bbb.html).
- **Trucks' share:** Trucks carry about 73.6% of US-Mexico freight by value. This is a secondary source citing BTS — [CXTMS blog](https://cxtms.com/blog/us-mexico-laredo-corridor-record-freight-volume-expansion-infrastructure-2026).
- **August 2026 (BTS):** Total North American transborder freight was $160.6B, up 22.6% YoY. Trucks moved $109.0B, up 29.8% YoY. Laredo was the #1 transborder port at about $37.7B in the month, followed by Ysleta TX ($15.8B), Detroit ($13.9B), Port Huron ($8.4B) and Buffalo-Niagara ($7.1B) — [BTS release, Aug 2026](https://www.bts.gov/newsroom/north-american-transborder-freight-valued-1606-billion-august-2026-226-august-2025); [TheTrucker](https://www.thetrucker.com/trucking-news/business/truck-freight-across-us-borders-jumps-nearly-30-in-august).
- **US-Mexico truck freight in Aug 2026:** reported at $73.9B. This figure comes from a secondary source and could not be checked against the BTS release — [CHR cross-border update Aug 2026](https://www.cvcvisions.com/en-us/chrglobal/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/cross-border/).
- **July 2026 (BTS):** Transborder freight was $157.6B, up 18.8% YoY — [BTS](https://www.bts.gov/newsroom/north-american-transborder-freight-valued-1576-billion-july-2026-188-july-2025).
- **April 2026:** US-Mexico freight was $86.0B (+23.4%), of which truck freight at the Mexican border was $64.2B — [Falcon Logistics blog citing BTS](https://falconlogisticsinc.com/blog/us-mexico-cross-border-trucking-2026/).
- **February 2026:** Mexico-US freight was $73.2B (+7.1% YoY) even though total transborder freight fell — [Mexico Business News](https://mexicobusiness.news/logistics/news/mexico-us-freight-rises-bilateral-trade-consolidates-lead).
- **Laredo capacity and drivers:** Uber Freight reports three Laredo capacity disruptions in 2026 tied to B-1 driver dependence. It estimates that about 25,000 B-1 drivers have lost visas since April 2025. Both are third-party estimates — via [Mexico Business News / search summary](https://mexicobusiness.news/logistics/news/mexico-drives-194-rise-north-american-freight-trade).
- **Current lane conditions:** Mexican light-vehicle production was roughly flat in H1 2026. Northbound lanes from Coahuila and Nuevo León show high load-to-truck ratios and firm rates. Shippers are adding transloading to their regular cross-border networks — [C.H. Robinson cross-border update, Aug 2026](https://www.cvcvisions.com/en-us/chrglobal/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/cross-border/).
- **USMCA review:** At the 1 July 2026 joint review the US "did not agree to renew the USMCA in its current form". That triggers annual reviews, with expiry on 1 July 2036 unless the parties extend. Preferential tariffs, rules of origin and dispute settlement are unchanged for now — [White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/usmca-2026-joint-review-united-states-declines-extend-agreement-triggering-annual); [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/07/mexico-ante-una-nueva-etapa-continuidad-del-tmec); [CRS R48787](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R48787).
- **Tariffs and talks:** USMCA-qualified goods remain exempt from the 23 July Section 301 action. Section 232 tariffs, and auto content rules in particular, are the main sticking point. USTR pointed to possible "interim arrangements" by end-2026. A US-Mexico round took place in Mexico City on 21–23 July, and sources conflict on whether it was completed or only scheduled — [AS/COA tracker](https://www.as-coa.org/articles/tracking-us-mexico-talks-usmca-review); [UC Davis MexAg July 2026](https://mexag.ucdavis.edu/reports/2026-07-08-usmca-review-report.html); [Thompson Hine](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/07/ustr-issues-statement-on-status-of-usmca-review/); [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/usmca-review-2026).
- **MVE (Manifestación de Valor Electrónica) timeline:** The deadline moved several times.
  - The first postponement went to 31 July 2026 (SAT/ANAM communiqué, 2 June) — [El Imparcial](https://www.elimparcial.com/dinero/2026/06/03/sat-amplia-hasta-el-31-de-julio-la-obligatoriedad-de-la-manifestacion-de-valor-electronica-que-cambia-para-importadores-y-empresas/); [ANAM comunicado 16-2026](https://www.anam.gob.mx/comunicado-prensa-conjunto-16-2026/).
  - It was then announced as mandatory from 1 August 2026 — [Comexyco](https://comexyco.com/2026/07/22/manifestacion-de-valor-electronica-sera-obligatoria-a-partir-del-1-de-agosto-de-2026/).
  - It was finally extended to 30 Sept, so E2-format transmission through VUCEM has been mandatory since **1 Oct 2026**, with documentary facilities until 31 Dec 2026 (3rd advance version of the 2nd RGCE 2026 modification resolution) — [I-Xport](https://i-xport.co/blog/anuncian-prorroga-de-la-manifestacion-de-valor-electronica-a-octubre); [Intertrade](https://www.intertradeaa.com/post/manifestaci%C3%B3n-de-valor-electr%C3%B3nica-2026-qu%C3%A9-cambia-con-la-pr%C3%B3rroga-hasta-el-30-de-septiembre); [The Logistics World](https://thelogisticsworld.com/planeacion-estrategica/aplaza-obligatoriedad-manifestacion-valor-electronica-claves-entender-sat-anam/).
  - One source mentions 1 Jan 2027 but is internally inconsistent.
- **Carta Porte:** Complemento Carta Porte version 3.1 has been the only valid version since 17 July 2024. It has applied to foreign-trade movements since 1 Jan 2024. I found no confirmed new 2026 version or change — [C.H. Robinson](https://chrobinson.com/resources/blog/navigating-complemento-carta-porte-3-1); [Sovos](https://sovos.com/mx/cambios-regulatorios/iva/mexico-el-sat-publico-la-version-3-1-de-la-carta-porte/). A July 2026 article from The Logistics World discusses a new complement version, but its content could not be confirmed — [The Logistics World 22 Jul 2026](https://thelogisticsworld.com/actualidad-logistica/sat-lanza-nueva-version-del-complemento-carta-porte).
- **Cross-border LTL growth:** Mordor Intelligence projects the US-Mexico cross-border freight market to grow at a 4.53% CAGR over 2026–2031. LTL is the fastest-growing road segment at a 5.04% CAGR, while FTL held 79.74% share in 2025. These are market-research estimates — [Mordor](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/us-mexico-cross-border-transport-market); [GlobeNewswire Jul 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/06/3322219/28124/en/experts-predict-soaring-demand-for-us-mexico-freight-transport-as-nearshoring-picks-up.html).
- **US LTL carriers at the border:** They are adding border service centers, partnerships and acquisitions. A common model consolidates US LTL into FTL to the border and deconsolidates onto Mexican LTL networks. This source is about 21 months old — [Sobel Network](https://sobelnet.com/expanding-opportunities-the-growing-role-of-us-ltl-providers-in-cross-border-trucking/).

### Inferences
- Value growth of about 20–30% YoY in mid-2026 is in current dollars and partly reflects pricing, tariffs and front-loading. Even so, cross-border trucking is the strongest-growing demand pool GLT touches.
- The MVE took effect on 1 Oct 2026, the documentary grace period ends 31 Dec 2026, and USMCA annual-review uncertainty continues. Together these create time-sensitive informational queries in Spanish ("manifestación de valor electrónica E2", "MVE VUCEM", "carta porte comercio exterior") and in English ("USMCA review 2026 impact shipping", "Mexico customs changes 2026"). GLT's Spanish-speaking MX/CO teams are a credible author base for that content.
- "Cross-border LTL Mexico" is a niche where FTL dominates and LTL grows faster, so there is room for a consolidated LTL-to-border offer and content.
- Laredo dominance plus B-1 driver disruptions suggest content and paid ads around "Laredo cross-border LTL/FTL", transloading and "capacity at Laredo".

- I found no 2026 data source sizing cross-border LTL specifically, in shipments or dollars.

- I could not confirm Carta Porte changes in 2026.

## US LTL market 2026 (size, rates, capacity, digital/instant quotes, forwarders using domestic LTL)

### Takeaway
US LTL is a market of about $52B with shrinking volumes but disciplined, rising pricing. 2025 revenue was $51.8B (−1.9%) and shipments fell 1.9%. In 2026, tightening truckload capacity is pushing freight toward LTL, and Q4 rate-per-pound is projected at record highs. The value proposition for buyers is price and service transparency, not cheap capacity.

### Cited Findings
- **2025 market size (SJ Consulting via JOC/Logistics Management Top 50):**
  - Total US LTL market was $51,807M in 2025, versus $52,803M in 2024 (−1.9%). The top 25 carriers had $47,297M — [JOC July 6, 2026 p34](https://magazine.joc.com/july-6-2026/0109987001782412674/p34); [SupplyChain247 Top 50](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/top_50_trucking_companies_strategy_separates_the_leaders/smc3).
  - FedEx Freight led at $8.78B, Old Dominion was second at $5.45B and Estes third at $4.99B.
  - Average daily shipments were about 649,800 (−1.9%).
  - The US LTL terminal count was 3,353, up 19% from 2023, as Yellow's terminals were absorbed — same sources.
- **Rates:**
  - AFS projects the TD Cowen/AFS LTL rate-per-pound index at 82.5% above its Jan 2018 baseline in Q4 2026, up 8.8% YoY — [Fleet Equipment Mag](https://www.fleetequipmentmag.com/q4-2026-freight-rates-fuel-capacity/).
  - Argon & Co expects effective increases of about 4–8% in 2026 — [Argon & Co](https://www.argonandco.com/us/news-insights/articles/2026-less-than-truckload-ltl-rate-outlook-discipline-versus-demand/).
  - An older C.H. Robinson outlook expected flat to low-single-digit increases — [C.H. Robinson Jan 2026](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/jan-2026-freight-market-update/na-ltl/).
  - Sources conflict on magnitude.
- **Volumes and capacity:**
  - National carriers report mid-single-digit tonnage declines.
  - Truckload capacity tightened in 2026 under federal CDL enforcement, which is pushing some freight to LTL — [Transport Topics July 2026](https://www.ttnews.com/articles/ltl-outlook-july-2026).
  - Peak-season tightness is expected in the Southeast and Texas — [ShipAtlantic Q4 outlook](https://www.shipatlantic.com/post/q4-2026-ltl-freight-outlook-what-shippers-should-expect).
  - Other 2026 overviews — [SupplyChain247 "LTL holds the line"](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/2026_market_update_ltl_holds_the_line); [Warp "State of LTL 2026"](https://www.wearewarp.com/research/state-of-ltl-2026) (vendor).
- **Digital and instant quotes:**
  - LTL is estimated at 15–20% digitized. This is a vendor estimate with no stated methodology — [FreightPulse](https://freightpulsehq.com/blog/digital-freight-marketplaces-reshaping-logistics-2026).
  - A BCG survey reportedly found that over 40% of shippers weigh a provider's AI capabilities in partner selection, while fewer than 10% treat it as mandatory. This was reported secondhand and not verified — [GoFreight blog](https://gofreight.com/blog/solution/unveiling-logistics-technology-trends-in-supply-chain-management.html).
  - Freightos (2025, ocean) found that half of the top forwarders offer instant quotes — [Freightos State of Online Freight 2025](https://www.freightos.com/freight-resources/state-of-online-freight-sales-2025/).
  - Among ShipStation customers, 6 in 10 call improving LTL a high priority. This is a self-selected sample — [ShipStation 2026](https://www.shipstation.com/guides/shipstation-2026-merchant-insights-report/).
- **Forwarders and domestic LTL:** The US freight forwarding brokerages and agencies industry is about $127.7B in 2026 — [IBISWorld](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/industry/freight-forwarding-brokerages-agencies/1209/). Forwarders and NVOCCs typically buy rather than own inland capacity, and some list domestic LTL/FTL services — [Interlog example](https://interlogusa.com/?p=6568).

### Inferences
- Rising LTL rates with soft volumes means shippers and forwarders are price-shopping. Queries like "LTL quote", "LTL rates 2026" and "freight class/NMFC changes" stay commercially valuable, and instant or near-instant quoting is a conversion lever.
- Low LTL digitization (if the 15–20% figure is right) leaves room for an LTL-for-brokers/forwarders program delivered through Magaya and API as a differentiator, rather than competing head-on with carrier portals.

### Gaps
- I found no independent 2026 survey on the share of LTL shipments quoted or booked online.
- I found no data on what share of forwarders buy domestic LTL through 3PLs versus carriers directly.
- I could not confirm 2026 YTD LTL revenue.

## AI search and agentic commerce in B2B logistics 2026 (buyer AI use, CTR impact, freight MCP servers, llms.txt)

### Takeaway
Generative AI is now part of almost every B2B purchase journey. Forrester reports 94% of buyers used it, and G2 reports 51% of software buyers start research in a chatbot more often than in Google. AI Overviews appear on about 43–48% of US Google queries and cut organic CTR sharply. Freight-booking MCP servers are live from Warp (April 2026) and Flexport (Sept 2026). llms.txt shows no measurable citation benefit.

### Cited Findings
- **Buyer use of generative AI:**
  - Forrester's 2026 Buyers' Journey Survey (about 18,000 buyers) found that 94% used generative AI in their latest purchase, up from 89% in 2025. This was reported secondhand — [MachineRelations](https://machinerelations.ai/research/b2b-ai-vendor-research-2026); [TestimonialStar](https://testimonialstar.com/resources/b2b-buyer-journey-llms-2026/).
  - G2 (March 2026, 1,076 software decision-makers) found that 51% start software research in an AI chatbot more often than in Google, up from 29% in April 2025. ChatGPT is the main chatbot for 63%. 69% chose a different vendor than planned because of chatbot guidance, and a third bought from a vendor first surfaced by AI. This covers software buyers, not freight — [SuperMarketers summary](https://www.supermarketers.ai/aeo/how-buyers-research-in-ai); [GEO Software Rankings](https://www.geosoftwarerankings.com/blog/ai-buyer-behavior-statistics-2026-45-sourced-data-points-on-how/).
  - Gartner found that 45% of B2B buyers used GenAI, mainly to gather vendor and product info — same summaries.
  - A multi-source analysis found that 73% of B2B buyers use AI tools in research — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/73-of-b2b-buyers-use-ai-tools-in-purchase-research-multi-source-analysis-finds-302733319.html).
- **AI Overviews prevalence:** AI Overviews appear on about 43% of US Google searches according to Similarweb's 2026 report, up from about 15% a year earlier. Semrush measures about 48%. Google has not confirmed either figure. AI Mode visits are growing faster than AI Overviews — [SE Roundtable](https://seroundtable.com/half-google-searches-ai-overviews-41780.html); [TechCrunch Jul 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/27/googles-ai-search-is-rapidly-becoming-the-default-new-data-shows/); [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/geo/ai-overviews-half-of-google-queries/).
- **CTR impact:**
  - Seer Interactive found that organic CTR on informational queries with an AI Overview fell from 1.76% to 0.61% (−61%) between June 2024 and Sept 2025.
  - Seer's follow-up showed a partial rebound to 2.36% in Feb 2026, still below the 3.82% no-AIO baseline.
  - Ahrefs (Dec 2025, 300k keywords) found AI Overviews cut position-1 CTR by 58%.
  - Pew (July 2025) found users click an organic result 8% of the time with an AIO versus 15% without one.
  - Sources — [Presenc.ai comparison](https://presenc.ai/research/ai-overviews-ctr-impact-2026); [Ideava 12 studies](https://ideava.com/insights/ai-overviews-ctr-decline/); [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/google-ai-overviews-the-end-of-traditional-ctr-and-how-to-adapt-in-2025).
- **AI referral traffic (Similarweb, via secondary summaries):**
  - AI referrals were about 0.9% of total visits in March 2026, up 5x YoY. AI referral traffic converts at about 7.1%, versus 7.8% for paid search and about 2.5x Google organic.
  - In a B2B panel, ChatGPT's share of AI referrals fell from about 89% to about 63% (May–Aug 2025 versus Mar–Apr 2026). Over the same periods Gemini rose from 2.4% to 10.6%, Perplexity from 3.1% to 7.3%, and Claude from 1.4% to 18.5%.
  - ChatGPT's share of overall GenAI web traffic fell from about 76% to about 52%.
  - Sources — [Salience on Similarweb 2026 GenAI landscape](https://salience.co.uk/insight/similarweb-2026-generative-ai-landscape); [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/ai-search-isnt-replacing-google-its-layering-on-top-similarweb-data/583378/); [mean.ceo compilation](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=11356).
- **Freight MCP servers (agentic booking):**
  - Warp's `warp-agent-mcp` was published on npm on 16 April 2026. It is MIT-licensed and quotes, books and tracks LTL, FTL, box truck and cargo van from Claude, Cursor and similar tools. Quoting needs no key; booking needs a Bearer key. Warp says it has 33 tools and calls itself the "first production freight MCP", which is a vendor claim. A directory lists 23 tools — [Warp MCP page](https://www.wearewarp.com/agents/mcp); [GitHub](https://github.com/warpfreight/warp-agent-mcp); [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/warp-agent-mcp); [mcpservers.org](https://mcpservers.org/servers/warpfreight/warp-agent-mcp).
  - Flexport launched an MCP server for shippers' AI agents to track, quote and book freight (FreightWaves, 29 Sept 2026) — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/flexport-mcp-server-ai-agents-freight-booking).
  - Freight Right: I found no source confirming a Freight Right MCP server.
- **llms.txt:**
  - About 10% of domains have adopted it (SE Ranking, about 300k domains). Ahrefs (June 2026, about 137k domains) reported adoption up 8.8x YoY, but 97% of llms.txt files got zero requests in May 2026.
  - No correlation with AI citations was found. 1 of 94,614 cited URLs was an llms.txt page (ALLMO).
  - Google's John Mueller compared it to the keywords meta tag.
  - One source claims OpenAI and Microsoft crawlers fetch llms-full.txt, which contradicts the above.
  - Sources — [WebYes](https://www.webyes.com/blogs/does-llms-txt-improve-rankings/); [Project LLMs.txt](https://projectllmstxt.com/blog/llms-txt-adoption-2026); [Limy](https://limy.ai/blog/llms-txt-in-2026-the-full-guide); [QuickSEO](https://quickseo.ai/blog/don-t-waste-time-on-llms-txt-in-2026).

### Inferences
- Forwarders and shippers researching an "LTL partner for freight forwarders" or a "Mexico cross-border carrier" will increasingly get a shortlist from ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, AI Overviews or AI Mode. If GLT is not named, it loses the deal before any click. G2 found 69% switched vendors due to chatbot guidance; this is software data and only directionally applicable to freight.
- AI referrals are small in volume but convert near paid-search levels, so they deserve tracking (GA4 referral segments for chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini, claude.ai).
- Top-of-funnel informational content (for example "what is LTL", "freight class") will lose clicks to AI Overviews. Effort should shift to quotable proprietary data, comparison and decision pages, and conversion pages.
- A GLT quote/book API or MCP endpoint (building on its existing TMS API) would put it in the same agentic channel Warp and Flexport just opened, and would be distinctive for a Miami 3PL. llms.txt costs little to add but should not be expected to drive results.

### Gaps
- I found no logistics- or freight-specific survey of buyer AI use; all figures are general B2B or software.
- No AI Mode referral volumes were found.

## GEO best practice 2026 (Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, ChatGPT search, Perplexity): what gets cited

### Takeaway
For commercial "best X" queries, AI engines draw most on ranked listicles and third-party review and comparison sources. They also draw on community and platform domains (Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, G2) and on brand mentions. Self-serving listicles still get cited but are being demoted by Google. Some studies find most citations come from brand-managed sources, so evidence conflicts.

### Cited Findings
- **Listicles:**
  - LumenGEO (300 commercial queries, 30 verticals) found ranked listicles were the dominant top-3 format for 53% of queries. Third-party content made up about two-thirds of the retrievable web, and review and comparison aggregators about half — [LumenGEO 2026](https://lumengeo.co/blog/state-of-ai-citations-2026).
  - Evertune reportedly found listicles at 63% across about 400M citations — via [Peec AI stats](https://peec.ai/ai-search-geo-statistics); [Masset](https://www.getmasset.com/resources/blog/write-a-listicle-ai-will-cite).
- **Self-promotional listicles:**
  - About 1 in 10 citations come from a vendor's own "best tools" page ranking itself first. Google AI Mode and Perplexity cite these at nearly 3x ChatGPT's rate — [Peec AI](https://peec.ai/ai-search-geo-statistics); [Promptwatch glossary](https://promptwatch.com/glossary/self-promotional-listicles).
  - Lily Ray found that when a brand's own listicle was cited in AI Overviews, the brand was left out of the recommendation 69% of the time. Her Sept 2026 update reports fewer such citations after a January 2026 Google suppression — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-overviews-cite-self-serving-listicles-recommend-competitors-480573).
- **Top cited domains (Peec AI, March 2026):** Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, Wikipedia, Forbes, G2, Yelp. G2 was reported as the most-cited software review platform across ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews — [Peec AI](https://peec.ai/ai-search-geo-statistics).
- **Conflicting evidence:** Yext's 6.8M-citation study found 86% of AI citations come from brand-managed sources such as websites and listings. Brand mentions reportedly correlate with citation more strongly than backlinks — [Position Digital stats (Oct 2026)](https://www.position.digital/blog/ai-seo-statistics/); [Digital Bloom GEO report](https://thedigitalbloom.com/learn/geo-traffic-optimization-report-2026/).
- **Academic basis:** The original GEO research from Princeton and others found that adding citations, statistics and quotations raises visibility in generative answers — [Wikipedia: GEO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_engine_optimization); [AIThinkerLab summary](https://aithinkerlab.com/generative-engine-optimization-2026/).

### Inferences
- What GLT likely needs, in priority order:
  1. Be included in third-party lists such as "best LTL providers for freight forwarders", "best cross-border Mexico carriers/3PLs", "Magaya integrations" and logistics directories.
  2. Build reviews and mentions on G2, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn and Reddit-type communities.
  3. Publish its own honest comparison pages, rather than "#1 = us" listicles, with fact-dense, stat-rich, quotable content: lane data, transit times, MVE and Carta Porte explainers in Spanish and English.
  4. Use structured data (Organization, Service, FAQ) and crawlable, non-JS-dependent pages.
- Because the brand-managed versus third-party evidence conflicts, both owned pages and earned mentions are needed.
